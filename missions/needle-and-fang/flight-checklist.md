# Needle and Fang native flight checklist

Record exact artifact hash, Git revision and DCS/module versions. Mock and archive checks are separate from these observations.

1. **Starts/site fit:** inspect both FARP pads, technicals and neutral cover structures in the Mission Editor/DCS. Confirm land, slope, forest/building/rotor clearance, zero-AGL hot starts and no collision. Sites are fixed; stop if unsuitable rather than silently relocating a prepoint target.
2. **Loadout/performance:** verify two Stingers, M3P/500 rounds, 181.4 kg fuel, 30 flares and PDU. Check gross weight/hover capability and reserve. Confirm Hind rocket/gun loadout with no ATGMs and its own stable hot start.
3. **Information:** labels off; enemy units absent from friendly-only F10. Fixed enemy-objective marker and 1T match the technical. MMS PREPT/R2 PPT/1T slews to the intended location; no waypoint forces an overflight. Hind position remains unknown.
4. **Native hunt:** wait on pad and confirm the Hind remains parked. Take off; observe its actual departure and attack on the friendly technical, then an encounter with the player. Record detection, maneuvering, weapons and damage rather than inferring them from release/task messages. Check both technicals can defend themselves.
5. **Complete normal sortie:** destroy the red technical while the Hind remains alive and verify immediate success. Separately kill only the Hind and verify the round continues. Test friendly technical destruction for immediate defeat; the first ordered target-death result must remain final. AI is told to hold fire after the result; released weapons cannot be recalled. Optional home recovery is not part of success.
6. **Rearm:** land and park on BLUE FARP, request Ground Crew > Rearm & Refuel, then confirm the Stinger and gun ammunition actually replenish. Inspect both sides' service trucks/canopies, coalition ownership and pad warehouse stock; repeat a red-pad service check through an editor test slot if needed. Destroying a logistics truck must not end the round.
7. **Loss/balance:** verify immediate failure on friendly technical destruction and failure on player loss. Assess whether launch timing, terrain and firepower allow meaningful interception/defense. Record any AI refusal, masked approach or unreachable target for revision. Do not certify the duel until it is flown.
