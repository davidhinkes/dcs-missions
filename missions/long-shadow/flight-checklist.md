# Long Shadow native flight checklist

Record artifact SHA-256, Git revision and DCS version. Keep observations separate from mock/archive results. Collect `[LONG_SHADOW]` lines from dcs.log.

1. **Editor/planner:** open in the Mission Editor; confirm no unknown-type errors (UAZ-469, Ural-375 ZU-23). In the Mission Planner confirm the convoy is hidden, the Kiowa has only its start point, and 1T is present. Check JUNIPER, the convoy start, 1T and Hama destination against the map's roads/river: 1T should sit on the M5 Orontes crossing.
2. **Start:** hot spawn on JUNIPER is level, clear of trees/buildings, no bounce. Log line `FARP JUNIPER height ... max 15 m height difference` should be ≤2.5 m and no "uneven" message. Check loadout (2 Hellfires, M3P/500, 30 flares, PDU, 181.4 kg), gross weight and hover.
3. **Pontiac:** visible in F10 orbiting east of the M5; "On station" message once. Report command answers before departure.
4. **Departure:** convoy appears at Homs north between H+4 and H+8 (log `Convoy activated`), on the road, not in buildings or water. Site-check log lines give nearest-road distances for each route point. Departure call says `by road` (if `direct`, record it: road pathing unavailable).
5. **Road following:** convoy takes the M5 through Talbiseh and over the crossing, not side streets; calls arrive in order 8…1 nm with sensible ETAs; "passing 1T" happens at the crossing; Hama calls follow.
6. **Strike:** identify the UAZ (third vehicle) through the MMS, Hellfire or gun it; success message and flag 960. Note whether the escorts engaged and whether the convoy reacted before the kill.
7. **Alert:** in a separate run shoot an escort first; confirm one "speeding up" call and visibly faster driving on the road to Hama.
8. **Escape:** let the convoy run; failure within 600 m of the Hama entrance.
9. **Fuel/reserve:** record fuel at the shot and on return to JUNIPER.
