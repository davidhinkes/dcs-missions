# Quiet Tide lessons

- Establish native deck compatibility before expanding the scenario. Distinct parking numbers and correct ship link IDs can pass archive validation while deck geometry or AI procedures still fail. Slot fit is a flight-check item, not a proven result.
- A moving ship invalidates fixed-position completion checks and ground-relative speed assumptions. Simulated recovery checks now use current vessel position and horizontal relative velocity for lead and transport; native inAir/deck behavior remains pending.
- Scripted team deployment is not infantry animation or a completed ground raid. Keep success language limited to insertion plus aircraft recovery.
- The checked-in unit/weather data was copied from the collection's Green Lantern templates for reproducibility. Quiet Tide does not read another mission's build or personal Saved Games data at runtime. No dependency upgrade was made.
